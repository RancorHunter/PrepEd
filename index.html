PHARMACOLOGICAL & PARENTERAL THERAPIES

A nurse is caring for a client receiving furosemide. Which laboratory value is most important to monitor?

Rationale

Furosemide is a loop diuretic that can cause potassium loss. Monitor serum potassium to identify hypokalemia and reduce dysrhythmia risk.
